# MASTER PROMPT — DARICY DANIELS ECOMMERCE WEBSITE

Act as a **senior luxury-fashion ecommerce website developer, UI/UX designer, brand digital strategist, full-stack engineer, conversion-rate optimization specialist, SEO/AEO specialist, and technical architect** with extensive experience building premium fashion ecommerce platforms.

Your task is to design and build a **complete, production-ready ecommerce website for DARICY DANIELS**, a luxury contemporary fashion brand.

Do not approach this as a basic template website. The result must feel like a **premium international fashion house** while remaining commercially effective, intuitive, fast, responsive, and easy to maintain.

---

## 1. BRAND IDENTITY

### Brand
**DARICY DANIELS**

### Brand positioning
Luxury Contemporary Fashion.

The website should communicate:

- Quiet luxury
- Sophistication
- Femininity
- Confidence
- Exclusivity
- Contemporary elegance
- Emotional storytelling
- Exceptional craftsmanship
- Modern African-inspired luxury where appropriate, without making the entire brand feel culturally stereotyped

The website should feel **editorial, refined, sensual, architectural, and expensive**.

Avoid:
- Generic fashion-template aesthetics
- Excessive gradients
- Overly flashy animations
- Cheap-looking ecommerce UI
- Excessive text
- Cluttered layouts
- Cartoonish icons
- Stock-photo aesthetics
- Overuse of gold
- Anything that makes the brand feel mass-market

The design should communicate luxury through **typography, whitespace, proportion, photography, composition, texture, and restraint** rather than excessive decoration.

# CRITICAL DESIGN INSTRUCTION

Do not make the website look like a generic ecommerce template with a burgundy color applied on top.

The **entire visual system** must communicate Daricy Daniels.

Think like a combination of:

**luxury fashion editorial + contemporary ecommerce + digital fashion house + high-conversion online boutique.**

Every design decision should answer:

> **Does this make Daricy Daniels feel more premium, memorable, desirable, and trustworthy?**

If the answer is no, redesign it.

When making design decisions, prioritize **luxury perception, usability, conversion, accessibility, performance, and scalability simultaneously.**

Do not sacrifice usability for aesthetics, and do not sacrifice luxury aesthetics for generic ecommerce conventions.

The final result should look like a brand that could confidently sell internationally.
